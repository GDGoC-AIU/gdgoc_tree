## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our community web pages seriously. If you discover a security vulnerability or a broken link/dependency within this project, please follow these steps:

1. **Do not create a public issue** regarding the vulnerability.
2. Send an email directly to **gdgoc.aiu@gmail.com** with details about the issue and steps to reproduce it.
3. We will review the report and respond within 48 hours.

Thank you for helping keep our community projects safe!
